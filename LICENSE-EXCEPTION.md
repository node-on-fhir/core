## 2. Definitions

**Covered Core** means the NodeOnFHIR software files distributed by the project under AGPL-3.0 and to which the copyright holders have validly applied this Additional Permission.

**Independent Extension** means a separately identifiable software package, plugin, module, or application that is developed independently of the Covered Core and interacts with it through published or otherwise supported interfaces, including package-loading, routing, configuration, event, or API interfaces. An Independent Extension does not include copied or modified portions of the Covered Core, except to the extent separately authorized by an applicable license.

**Permitted Combination** means the loading, linking, execution, integration, or distribution of the Covered Core together with one or more Independent Extensions through those interfaces.
